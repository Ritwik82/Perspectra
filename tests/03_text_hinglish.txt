Ek ajeeb sa observation hai social media ko lekar

Pichhle kuch time se maine notice kiya hai ki mere friend circle mein kaafi log Instagram aur baaki social media apps use karte hain, lekin unmein se kuch log bolte rehte hain ki woh mentally tired feel karte hain. Kabhi koi bolta hai ki sabki life dekh ke lagta hai ki woh khud kuch achieve nahi kar raha, toh kabhi koi bolta hai ki bas reels scroll karte karte ek ghanta nikal jaata hai aur baad mein samajh bhi nahi aata ki dekha kya tha.

Lekin interesting baat ye hai ki kuch log social media ko genuinely useful bhi maante hain. Unke liye ye friends ke contact mein rehne ka tareeka hai, naye interests discover karne ka source hai, aur kabhi kabhi difficult time mein kisi community ka part feel karne ka bhi ek way hai.

Maine ye bhi notice kiya hai ki same person kabhi social media use karke kaafi entertained feel karta hai, aur kabhi wahi app use karne ke baad time waste hone ka regret karta hai. Kuch log apna screen time limit karne ki koshish karte hain, kuch notifications off kar dete hain, aur kuch log bina kisi problem ke kaafi time spend karte rehte hain.

Ab mujhe genuinely samajhna hai ki ismein actual role kis cheez ka hai. Kya difference platform ke design mein hai, hum kis type ka content dekhte hain, hum app use karne ke liye kyun kholte hain, ya phir hamari existing mood aur circumstances bhi matter karti hain?

Aur ek aur cheez: jo log social media ke baare mein negative experiences share karte hain, kya woh un experiences ko zyada notice karte hain kyunki unka impact strong hota hai? Ya phir jo log casually use karte hain aur koi issue feel nahi karte, woh is topic par baat hi kam karte hain?

Mere paas koi proper data nahi hai, bas apne aas-paas ki observations hain. Isliye main ye assume nahi kar raha ki social media sabke liye good hai ya bad. Bas ye samajhna interesting hoga ki different logon ke experiences itne different kyun ho sakte hain, aur kaunsi information se is question ka better answer mil sakta hai.
